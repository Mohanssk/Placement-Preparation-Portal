# Placement Preparation Portal: End-User Guide

Welcome to the **Placement Preparation Portal**! This platform is designed to be your all-in-one hub for cracking campus placements and landing your dream job. Instead of searching through scattered WhatsApp groups and Drive links, everything you need is organized right here.

Here is a quick guide on how to navigate and get the most out of the platform.

---

## 🚀 Getting Started

1. **Create an Account:** Click the "Sign Up" button to create your student profile. 
2. **Log In:** Use your credentials to access your personalized dashboard, where you can track your saved jobs, preparation progress, and recent placement notices.

---

## 🛠️ Core Features & How to Use Them

### 1. ATS Resume Analyzer (Standout Feature)
Before applying for any role, make sure your resume will pass the company's automated screening software (ATS).
* **How to use:** Navigate to the **Resume Scanner** page. Upload your resume (PDF format), and paste the text of the Job Description (JD) you are applying for.
* **What you get:** The system will calculate a **Match Score** and instantly tell you which crucial keywords you are missing so you can add them before submitting your application.

### 2. The AI Chat Assistant
Stuck on a tricky interview question or need a quick explanation of a coding concept? 
* **How to use:** Look for the **"Chat with AI"** button at the bottom of the left sidebar. Click it to open the chat drawer.
* **What you can ask:** "Explain polymorphism simply," "Give me a polite way to ask for feedback after an interview," or "What are the common HR questions for a backend developer?"

### 3. Company Directory
Research your target companies before they visit the campus.
* **How to use:** Click on **Companies** to view profiles of organizations that hire from your college. You can check their historical hiring trends, eligibility criteria (like minimum GPA), and the specific roles they offer.

### 4. Preparation Hub (Aptitude & Coding)
Your structured roadmap for technical and non-technical rounds.
* **How to use:** Go to the **Preparation Hub**. Switch between the "Aptitude" and "Coding" tabs. Here, you will find categorized resources, high-yield topics (like Arrays, Dynamic Programming), and direct links to practice platforms.

### 5. Interview Experiences Forum
Learn from the seniors who have already been through the process.
* **How to use:** Browse the **Experiences** feed to read first-hand accounts of technical and HR rounds. You can filter posts by Company Name, Role, or Outcome (Selected/Rejected) to find the exact insights you need.

### 6. Notice Board & Alerts
Never miss a deadline. 
* **How to use:** Check the **Notice Board** on your dashboard regularly for real-time announcements from the placement cell, registration links, and upcoming drive dates.

---

## 💡 Quick Tips for Success
* **Scan Every Time:** Always run your resume through the ATS Analyzer for *each specific job* you apply to. Tailoring your resume is the fastest way to get shortlisted.
* **Learn from Rejections:** Pay special attention to the "Rejected" tags in the Interview Experiences forum. Knowing what mistakes to avoid is just as valuable as knowing what to do right.
* **Use the AI as a Tutor:** Don't just ask the AI for answers; ask it to *explain* concepts you don't understand while you are practicing coding questions.

**Good luck with your preparation!**
